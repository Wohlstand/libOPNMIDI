A General MIDI bank that attempts to replicate sounding of the NEC OPNA driver
for the Windows 95/98 for Japanese computers of the NEC PC98 family.

This bank is a collaborative effort of Vitaliy Novichkov (https://github.com/Wohlstand)
and  馬頭猫 (https://github.com/EriczjrWinter) who made a reverse-engineering
work of the original NECOPN.DRV driver to extract the melodic set.

The complete percussion set was built using different banks (xg.wopn and
fmmidi.wopn) as a base.

License for this bank - MIT

To edit this bank and other banks in WOPN format, you can use this editor
which I created for that: https://github.com/Wohlstand/OPN2BankEditor
