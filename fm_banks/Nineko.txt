This is the default bank from the Nineko's MIDI2VGM VB6-written utility.
It contained no drums, and therefore, the drumkit was imported from the
"gems-fmlib-gmize.wopn" bank.

Original goes from the here: https://vgmrips.net/forum/viewtopic.php?t=1929
(The midi2vgm 2612 entry)
